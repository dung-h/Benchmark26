# Third-party provenance and distribution limits

Preserve attribution to the original Ma–Li, Qonductor and QPack datasets/methods and MQTBench reconstruction software. Public availability is not a blanket redistribution license. No new license is imposed here on upstream data/code or on the user's work.

The retained source [distribution review](docs/benchmark_release_plan.md#s5--rights-evidence-and-owner-decisions) records the unresolved Ma–Li data/helper permissions, Qonductor MIT software notice (Dmitry Lugovoy, 2023) versus dataset permissions, QPack MPL-2.0 software versus measurement-corpus terms, and MQTBench MIT generator versus runtime labels. This candidate is for local/private review preparation and is not an approved public release. Upstream checkout/asset acquisition and final notices remain explicit release tasks.
