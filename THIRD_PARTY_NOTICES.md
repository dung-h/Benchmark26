# Third-party provenance and distribution limits

Preserve attribution to the original Ma–Li, Qonductor and QPack datasets/methods and MQTBench reconstruction software. Public availability is not a blanket redistribution license. No new license is imposed here on upstream data/code or on the user's work.

Upstream software licenses and measurement-data permissions are separate.
Qonductor software carries an MIT notice (Dmitry Lugovoy, 2023); QPack software
uses MPL-2.0; MQTBench generator software uses MIT. These software licenses do
not establish redistribution rights for their associated runtime measurements.
Ma–Li data and helper permissions also require separate review before redistribution.
Consult the licenses and notices at the pinned upstream revisions in
`upstream/data_sources.json` when acquiring or distributing third-party material.
